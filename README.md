# RAEED

RAEED is a private mobile and web platform that connects the three roles around each enrolled child:

- **Parents**
- **Educators** (مؤطرين / *mo'atirin*)
- **Executives** (مشرفين / *moshrifin*)

The goal is to give the association a single, safeguarded place to run its activities and keep parents informed.

## Roles

| Role | Who | What they do on the platform |
|---|---|---|
| Parent | Guardian of an enrolled child | Follows their child's attendance, homework, materials, and messages |
| Educator (mo'atirin) | Runs sessions for a group | Confirms presence, records attendance, assigns homework, shares materials, posts to the Memories Wall |
| Executive (moshrifin) | Oversees the association | Manages categories/groups, views dashboards and audit logs, sends announcements |

## Release plan

### MVP (first release)

- Categories (فئات), groups, and weekly sessions
- Presence confirmation and attendance tracking, with instant absence alerts
- Homework tracking
- Session materials kept available for later review
- Safeguarded messaging and targeted announcements
- Private Memories Wall for activity photos and videos
- Executive dashboard and audit logs

### V1 (second release)

- Camps and events
- Enrollment
- Fees
